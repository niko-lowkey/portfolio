How should I prepare for my appointment?

Arrive 10 minutes early to complete any new-patient forms, bring a photo ID and your insurance card if applicable, and brush your teeth beforehand. If you're coming for a specific concern, jot down any symptoms and when they started.

Which services and insurance do you accept?

We offer general, cosmetic, and emergency dentistry and accept most major insurance plans. Call us and we'll verify your coverage before your visit.

What is your cancellation and reschedule policy?

Please let us know at least 24 hours before your appointment so we can offer your time to another patient. Rescheduling is always free.

Do you handle dental emergencies?

Yes. We hold same-day openings for urgent cases like severe pain, chipped or knocked-out teeth, and swelling. Call us as soon as possible.

What should I expect on my first visit?

A gentle exam, digital X-rays if needed, a professional cleaning, and a friendly conversation about your goals and a personalized treatment plan.
