Crownwell Dental Clinic: from first click to follow up

A free guide funnel, a booking calendar, a patient pipeline and four connected workflows that confirm, remind and follow up automatically.

ProjectCrownwell Dental Clinic (demo)
PlatformGoHighLevel
Built2 funnels, 4 workflows

01The brief

A dental clinic has to do several things at once: catch interest from people who are not ready to book, make booking simple for those who are, remind patients before a visit, and stay in touch when plans change.

This project builds one organised system for all of it. Crownwell is a fictional clinic created for this portfolio demonstration.

02The plan

Every person who enters the system gets one opportunity in a pipeline called Patient Journey. Four workflows move that opportunity between five stages and send the right emails at the right time.

Visitor→ Free guide or booking→ Pipeline stage→ Staff set visit status→ Follow up
New Lead Appointment Booked Appointment Completed Cancelled / No Show Lead Nurture Completed

03Step 1: the lead enters their name and email

The first journey, from a visitor asking for the free guide to a finished follow up.

  1. The formThe visitor enters their full name and email on the opt in page and ticks the consent box.
  2. The thank you pageIt confirms the guide is on its way and asks them to check the inbox.
  3. The guide email and a team alertA branded email delivers the PDF guide, and the team is told a new potential patient has asked for it.
  4. The pipelineThe workflow looks for an existing record. If there is none, it creates a New Lead opportunity.
  5. The follow upThree friendly emails go out over about three weeks, each with a link to book.
  6. The finishIf the lead books at any point, the follow up stops. If not, they move to Lead Nurture Completed.

04What was built

  • A free guide funnel: opt in page and thank you page
  • An appointment funnel: landing page, booking page and thank you page
  • A booking calendar with 5 services in 3 categories
  • A Patient Journey pipeline with 5 stages
  • 12 tags that track lead source, patient type, appointment status and outcome
  • 4 workflows: guide delivery and follow up, booking and reminders, after the visit, and cancellations and no shows
  • Branded emails and internal team alerts
Portfolio demonstration. Crownwell Dental Clinic and its contact details are fictional, and no client results are claimed.
