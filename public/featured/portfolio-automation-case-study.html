Portfolio Lead Automation · Make.com

Portfolio inquiries that manage themselves

Instead of checking my email for every new lead, the contact form on this portfolio now starts two Make.com scenarios. They log each lead in a Google Sheet CRM, reply with a booking link, keep the status up to date as people book, reschedule or cancel, and alert me on Telegram.

The idea

What it does

Every inquiry follows the same path, and most of it needs no input from me. The two steps that need a human decision, sending the proposal and choosing the outcome, stay manual on purpose.

1
A visitor sends the form AutomaticMake receives it and logs the lead in the Google Sheet with the status New.
2
The lead gets a reply and I get a ping AutomaticAn email with my booking link goes to the lead, and Cotton, my Telegram bot, tells me about the inquiry.
3
The lead books a discovery call AutomaticA 30 minute Google Meet call on Cal.com. The sheet is updated to Booked and Cotton alerts me. Rescheduling and cancelling are tracked the same way.
4
Cal.com sends reminders AutomaticOne day before and one hour before the call, using Cal.com’s default reminder content on the free tier.
5
I hold the call and send the proposal ManualThe proposal is written and sent by me after the call.
6
I mark the outcome ManualWon, Maybe/Later or Lost. Choosing Maybe/Later starts a nurture sequence at 3, 7 and 14 days.
The automation

Two Make.com scenarios

One handles the form. The other listens to Cal.com and routes each booking event to the right update.

Scenario 1: Portfolio Website Form Submit

Runs when a visitor submits the contact form.

TriggerA visitor submits the contact form on the portfolio. A custom webhook in Make receives it.
FilterThe Real Person Only filter sits between the webhook and the sheet, so only real submissions continue.
CRMGoogle Sheets Add a Row: details saved to the CRM sheet with the status New.
EmailGmail Send an email: a personal reply with the discovery call booking link goes to the lead.
NotifyCotton, my Telegram bot, sends me the new inquiry: name, email and message.

Scenario 2: Booking Appointment in Cal.com

Runs when someone books, reschedules or cancels a call. A router sends each event down its own route.

TriggerCal.com sends a webhook to Make when someone books, reschedules or cancels.
RouterMake sends the event down one of three routes: Booked, Cancelled or Reschedule.
CRMSearch Rows finds the lead in the sheet, then Update a Row writes the new details and status.
NotifyCotton sends a Telegram message that matches the event, with the name, email and call time or reason.
RouteSheet updateTelegram alert
BookedCall date and time saved, status set to Booked. A Validate Record filter sits between the search and the update.Name, email and the call time
RescheduleCall time updated to the new slot, status set to RescheduledName, email and the new time
CancelledStatus set to Lost and the cancellation reason saved in NotesName, email and the reason

After the call: the manual decision and the nurture drip

Not a Make scenario. This part is a Google Apps Script plus my own decision.

TriggerThe discovery call is held on Google Meet and I send the proposal myself.
DecisionI update the lead’s status in the sheet by hand: Won, Maybe/Later or Lost.
WaitIf Maybe/Later, a Google Apps Script starts the nurture sequence.
EmailFollow-up emails go out at 3 days, 7 days and 14 days.

Beyond the happy path

Edge cases the system handles

SituationWhat happens
Lead cancelsThe sheet status becomes Lost, the reason is saved in Notes and Cotton tells me why.
Lead reschedulesThe existing row is found and its call time and status are updated, then I get an alert with the new time.
Lead submits but never booksThey stay at New with the booking link already sent, and the reply invites them to write back if no time works.
Lead is interested but not readyI mark Maybe/Later and the 3, 7 and 14 day sequence takes over. Each email includes a simple way to opt out.
Non-genuine submissionA Real Person Only filter sits between the form webhook and the sheet.
ScopeNot part of this build: no-show handling. Proposals and final outcomes are handled by hand.

One sheet is the record. Every inquiry, booking and change updates it by itself. I only set the statuses that need a human call.

A clear record of every lead, and an alert or email ready for every moment that follows.
